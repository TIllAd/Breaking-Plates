[h1]Breaking Plates[/h1]
A [i]Breaking Bad[/i] parody for PlateUp! Cook [b]Blue Rock Candy[/b] in lab flasks, smash it up and serve it in little bags.
It's just sugar. Probably.

[h2]How to cook[/h2]
[list]
[*]Take a [b]lab flask[/b] and add [b]sugar[/b] and [b]water[/b]
[*][b]Cook[/b] it → blue syrup
[*]Let it [b]cool down[/b] (a [b]freezer[/b] or the [b]Superlab[/b] is much faster) to get crystals
[*][b]Interact[/b] on a counter to smash the crystals → 5 portions
[*][b]Portion[/b] it and combine with a [b]candy bag[/b] to serve – no plates needed
[/list]

[h2]Cards[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Blue Rock Candy[/b] – "We need to cook."
[*][b]99.1% Purity[/b] – recrystallise your candy (water in, cook and cool again) for pure, white crystals. "A higher purity means a greater yield."
[*][b]Chili P[/b] – a chopped jalapeño in the bag, marked with a chili clip. "Chili P is my signature!"
[*][b]Sugar-Free[/b] – cook with stevia, marked with a stevia sprig. "I'll need more stevia."
[*][b]On a Stick[/b] – rock candy lollis straight from the flask. "Yeah, science!"
[*][b]Mass Production[/b] – groups of up to 5 order a full flask, in every variant you sell. "I'm in the empire business."
[*][b]Schraderbräu[/b] – home-brewed beer as a side. "Home-brewed to silky perfection."
[*][b]Birthday Bacon[/b] – fry some bacon as a side. "Fifty-two."
[*][b]Breaking Even[/b] – fewer customers, earn 25% less money. "Better call Saul!"
[/list]

[h2]Superlab[/h2]
An oven turned lab reactor: cooks everything and cools your flasks, with a lamp and a timer.

[h2]Outfits[/h2]
A yellow hazmat suit with hood and visor, and a gas mask with big round lenses.

[h2]Decorations[/h2]
11 decorations across all four styles: periodic table, Gale's lab notebook, Marie's purple armchair, a pink teddy bear, lily of the valley, Hank's mineral collection, a money stack, yellow barrels (just decoration, honest), a Los Pollitos Hermanos sign, a Better Call Saul! poster and a fly that won't leave.

[h2]Colourblind support[/h2]
Every flask stage and bag variant has a colourblind label (Su, SF, W, Sy, Cr, Sh, Re, 99, St, Ch, Be).
