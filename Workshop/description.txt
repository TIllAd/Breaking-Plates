[h1]Breaking Plates[/h1]
A [i]Breaking Bad[/i] parody for PlateUp! Cook [b]Blue Rock Candy[/b] in lab flasks, smash it up and serve it in little bags.
It's just sugar. Probably.

[h2]How to cook[/h2]
[list]
[*]Take a [b]lab flask[/b] (only 4 – put them back!) and add [b]sugar[/b] and [b]water[/b]
[*][b]Cook[/b] it → blue syrup
[*]Let it [b]cool[/b] off the heat – a [b]freezer[/b] is much faster → crystals
[*][b]Smash[/b] the crystals by hand on a counter → 5 portions
[*]Bag a portion and serve – no plates needed
[/list]

[h2]Cards[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Blue Rock Candy[/b] – "Say my name."
[*][b]99.1% Purity[/b] – the Crystal Purifier makes pure, white crystals. "I am the one who knocks."
[*][b]Chili P[/b] – a chopped jalapeño in the bag, marked with a chili clip. "Chili P is my signature!"
[*][b]Sugar-Free[/b] – cook with stevia, marked with a stevia sprig. "Stevia."
[*][b]On a Stick[/b] – rock candy lollis straight from the flask. "Yeah, science!"
[*][b]Mass Production[/b] – groups order a whole flask, in every variant you sell. "I'm in the empire business."
[*][b]Schraderbräu[/b] – home-brewed beer as a side. "Jesus, Marie!"
[*][b]Breaking Even[/b] – fewer customers, but 25% less money each day. "Better call Saul!"
[/list]

[h2]Decorations[/h2]
Periodic table poster, yellow barrels (just decoration, honest) and a Los Pollitos Hermanos sign.
